Comprehensive professional interior design guidelines, spatial rules, and AI room-planning specifications for all 8 supported room types.

## Supported Room Categories & Detailed Design Specifications

### 1. Living Room
* **Spatial Focus**: Social gathering, media entertainment, and comfortable conversation.
* **Furniture**:
  * *Essential*: Sofa set or modular sectional sofa, central coffee table (45 cm height), low-profile TV console.
  * *Optional / Accent*: Swivel accent armchair, matching C-side tables, glass-front curio display cabinet.
* **Appliances**: 55"–65" wall-mounted smart television, integrated soundbar or acoustic audio system.
* **3-Tier Lighting**:
  * *Ambient*: Dimmable 2700K warm-white recessed ceiling LED downlights.
  * *Task*: Sculptural reading floor lamp beside the seating nook.
  * *Accent*: Indirect warm LED bias lighting behind the TV console to prevent screen eye strain.
* **Decorations & Soft Furnishings**: Floor-to-ceiling sheer linen curtains with blackout blinds, large area rug (min. 2.4 m × 3.0 m) anchoring front furniture legs, scatter cushions, framed canvas wall art, decorative mirrors, and indoor plants (*Fiddle-leaf fig*, *Monstera*).
* **Storage Units**: Low-profile media console with concealed cable management raceways, floating display wall shelves.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Natural white oak or American walnut timbers, textured bouclé or semi-aniline leather upholstery, matte black and brushed brass hardware.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  * **60% Dominant**: Warm ivory or alabaster white on walls and ceilings.
  * **30% Secondary**: Earthy taupe, greige, or soft charcoal on upholstery and rug.
  * **10% Accent**: Terracotta, champagne brass, or forest green in cushions and metal accessories.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: 45 cm (18") clearance between sofa and coffee table; 1.8–2.4 m viewing distance to TV; 80–90 cm primary entry walking corridor.
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: Flat-pack laminate media console, durable polyester-blend 3-seater sofa, printed area rug.
  * *Premium / Luxury*: Custom solid oak media millwork, Italian top-grain leather sectional, hand-knotted wool area rug.
* **AI Visualization Prompt**:
  > *"Architectural photography of an open-concept Modern Living Room, warm alabaster white walls, natural oak timber floors, low-profile modular sectional in warm greige bouclé, round marble coffee table, 65-inch television on minimalist oak console, floor-to-ceiling sheer linen drapery, fiddle-leaf fig in ceramic pot, warm indirect 2700K cove lighting, 8k resolution, cinematic lighting, photorealistic interior, Unreal Engine 5 render."*

### 2. Bedroom
* **Spatial Focus**: Restorative sleep, calm atmosphere, and functional wardrobe access.
* **Furniture**:
  * *Essential*: Queen-size bed (1.6 m × 2.0 m) with ergonomic mattress and padded headboard, 1 or 2 bedside nightstands.
  * *Optional / Accent*: Dressing table with vanity mirror, compact study/laptop desk with task chair.
* **Appliances**: Silent inverter split AC (1.0–1.5 Ton) with indirect airflow, focused bedside reading lamps.
* **3-Tier Lighting**:
  * *Ambient*: Dimmable warm 2700K ceiling fixture.
  * *Task*: Independent bedside reading sconces mounted at 105 cm height.
  * *Accent*: Automatic sensor LED strip lighting inside wardrobe closets.
* **Decorations & Soft Furnishings**: Dual-track curtains (sheers + blackout drapes), plush bedside wool runner rug extending 65 cm past mattress, washed linen bedding, minimalist framed prints.
* **Storage Units**: Full-height 2-to-3 door wardrobe with internal dividers, bedside nightstand drawers, under-bed storage bins.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Light birch, blonde pine, or natural oak; breathable washed linen and cotton percale; brushed nickel or matte black hardware.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  * **60% Dominant**: Soft oatmeal or warm bone white.
  * **30% Secondary**: Muted sage green or cashmere grey.
  * **10% Accent**: Brushed brass, burnt amber, or slate graphite.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: Bed placed in the command position facing doorway; 65–75 cm perimeter walkway on both sides of the mattress.
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: Upholstered platform bed, ready-to-assemble 2-door wardrobe, blended cotton linens.
  * *Premium / Luxury*: Solid oak floating bed with integrated nightstands, custom floor-to-ceiling wardrobe with tinted glass doors, pure Belgian flax linen.
* **AI Visualization Prompt**:
  > *"Serene Scandinavian Bedroom interior, warm oatmeal walls, natural light blonde oak platform bed with padded linen headboard, dual oak floating nightstands with brass reading sconces, sheer linen curtains, plush wool area rug, muted sage green bedding throw, quiet morning sunlight, clutter-free, photorealistic 8k, architectural magazine editorial."*

### 3. Master Bedroom
* **Spatial Focus**: Master suite luxury, spatial symmetry, walk-in dressing, and private lounge sanctuary.
* **Furniture**:
  * *Essential*: King-size bed (1.8 m × 2.0 m) with orthopedic mattress and statement architectural headboard, two symmetric bedside nightstands.
  * *Optional / Accent*: Dressing vanity with full-length LED mirror, wingback lounge armchair with footstool, foot-of-bed bench, wall TV unit.
* **Appliances**: 55" wall-mounted 4K smart TV with concealed conduit, whisper-quiet inverter split AC (1.5–2.0 Ton), motorized blackout blinds.
* **3-Tier Lighting**:
  * *Ambient*: Multi-zone dimmable perimeter cove ceiling lighting.
  * *Task*: High-CRI (95+) vanity mirror illumination and wardrobe LED hanging rails.
  * *Accent*: Suspended mini-pendant drops paired with 1W focused reading spots.
* **Decorations & Soft Furnishings**: 8x10 ft hand-tufted wool rug under King bed, 500-thread-count cotton sateen bedding, velvet accent cushions, floor-to-ceiling pinch-pleat blackout drapery, oversized canvas art.
* **Storage Units**: Walk-in closet or full-wall wardrobe with valet rods, twin 3-drawer nightstands, vanity jewelry drawers.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Fumed oak, dark American walnut, fluted acoustic panels, velvet, bouclé, brushed champagne bronze or graphite hardware.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:

  * **30% Secondary**: Slate taupe, charcoal, or deep greige.
  * **10% Accent**: Champagne gold, brushed brass, or emerald green.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: Symmetrical 60–75 cm bedside access corridors; 90 cm clear swing depth in front of wardrobes; dedicated 1.0 m seating circle for lounge armchair.
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: King storage platform with channel tufting, modular sliding wardrobe, dimmable LED ring pendants.

### 4. Kitchen
* **Spatial Focus**: Golden Work Triangle (Sink, Stove, Refrigerator), high-capacity storage, food prep ergonomics.
* **Furniture & Cabinetry**:
  * *Essential*: Modular base cabinets with heavy-duty tandem drawers, overhead wall cabinets with hydraulic lift-up doors, continuous stone countertop with matching backsplash.
  * *Optional / Modular*: Kitchen prep island (1.2 m × 0.65 m) with hardwood/quartz top and breakfast counter stools.
* **Appliances**: Built-in 3 or 4-burner gas stove or induction cooktop, high-suction (1200+ m³/hr) baffle-filter chimney hood, double-door refrigerator, built-in microwave/oven tower, optional dishwasher.
* **Plumbing Fixtures**: Double-bowl stainless steel or granite composite sink with swan-neck pull-out spray mixer faucet.
* **Storage Units**:
  * *Cutlery & Spoon Drawers*: 3-tier shallow drawers with partitioned organizer trays for spoons, forks, knives, and kitchen tools.
  * *Daily Essentials Cupboard*: Eye-level overhead wall cabinet for daily spices, cups, plates, and breakfast ingredients.
  * *Tall Pantry Tower*: 6-tier pull-out wire pantry tower for grocery storage.
  * *Under-Sink Cabinet*: Waterproof pull-out caddy for cleaning chemicals and RO water filtration.
* **3-Tier Lighting**:
  * *Task*: High-CRI 3500K–4000K continuous profile LED strips under upper wall cabinets directly illuminating countertops.
  * *Ambient*: Recessed glare-free ceiling downlights.
  * *Accent*: Warm drop pendants centered 75–85 cm above the island counter.
* **Decorations & Ventilation**: Window herb planters, magnetic knife strip, wooden spice block, high-suction exhaust chimney.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Engineered quartz or sintered stone countertops, marine-grade BWP plywood shutters with acrylic or anti-fingerprint laminate, 304-grade stainless steel fixtures.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  * **60% Dominant**: Alabaster white or light dove grey on upper cabinets and ceiling.
  * **30% Secondary**: Warm oak or charcoal matte laminate on lower cabinets and island.
  * **10% Accent**: Brushed brass or matte black pulls, mixer faucet, and chimney casing.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: Work Triangle perimeter between 4.0 m and 7.5 m (each leg 1.2–2.7 m); 105–120 cm (42–48") walkway clearance around island.
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: High-pressure laminate shutters, granite countertop, standalone gas stove, stainless steel single sink.
  * *Premium / Modular*: Seamless Calacatta quartz countertops with waterfall island, Blum servo-drive motorized drawers, touch induction hob, integrated dishwasher.
* **AI Visualization Prompt**:
  > *"Ultra-modern Kitchen interior, seamless white quartz countertops, waterfall island with prep surface and bar stools, matte dove grey and warm oak flat-panel cabinetry, under-cabinet warm task strip lighting, stainless steel double basin sink with swan-neck faucet, glass induction cooktop with sleek ducted chimney, clean ergonomic work triangle, 8k photorealistic architectural photography."*

### 5. Bathroom
* **Spatial Focus**: Personal wellness, wet/dry zoning, slip-resistant flooring, and shadow-free grooming.
* **Furniture & Vanities**:
  * *Essential*: Washbasin with floating water-resistant vanity cabinet, LED-backlit mirror or mirrored medicine cabinet.
  * *Optional / Accent*: Tall linen storage cabinet, open teak ladder shelf.
* **Sanitaryware & Fixtures**: Rimless wall-hung toilet with concealed dual-flush cistern, walk-in shower area or freestanding soaking tub, 10mm toughened frameless glass partition, thermostatic rain showerhead and diverter mixer, stainless steel towel racks and robe hooks.
* **Lighting & Moisture Ventilation**:
  * *Task*: Vertical wall sconces flanking mirror at eye level (~165 cm) to eliminate face shadows.
  * *Ambient*: IP65 waterproof recessed ceiling downlights.
  * *Accent*: Indirect warm cove lighting under floating vanity.
  * *Ventilation*: High-velocity low-noise moisture exhaust fan (150+ CFM).
* **Flooring & Wall Tiles**: Anti-slip (R10/R11 rating) matte porcelain floor tiles with 1:50 slope towards channel drain; full-height waterproof wall cladding.
* **Storage Units**: Deep under-basin drawers with U-cutouts, recessed shower shampoo niche, mirrored medicine cabinet.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Marine-grade BWP plywood with waterproof laminate, sintered stone counter, 10mm toughened safety glass, PVD-coated matte black, brushed brass, or chrome fixtures.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  * **60% Dominant**: Warm off-white or bone travertine wall tiles.
  * **30% Secondary**: Natural slate or ribbed timber vanity finish.
  * **10% Accent**: Matte black or brushed brass hardware, faucets, and mirror frames.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: Minimum 75 cm clear zone in front of basin and toilet; vanity counter height at 85–90 cm; bottom of mirror 15–20 cm above faucet.
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: Wall-mounted single basin vanity, chrome tap, framed partial glass screen, ceramic tiles.
  * *Premium / Luxury*: Floating dual-basin quartz vanity, thermostatic concealed diverter, freestanding composite stone bathtub, smart anti-fog mirror.
* **AI Visualization Prompt**:

### 6. Dining Room
* **Spatial Focus**: Shared dining, hospitality, proportioned table layout, and atmospheric illumination.
* **Furniture**:
  * *Essential*: Dining table scaled to room size (2, 4, 6, or 8 persons), ergonomic matching dining chairs.
  * *Optional / Accent*: Low sideboard credenza, glass-front crockery cabinet, serving trolley / bar cart.
* **3-Tier Lighting**:
  * *Focal / Task*: Statement pendant light or chandelier centered 75–85 cm (30–34") directly above table surface.
  * *Ambient*: Dimmable perimeter warm wall sconces (2700K).
* **Decorations & Soft Furnishings**: Flatweave or low-pile wool rug extending at least 65 cm beyond table edges, woven linen table runner, ceramic centerpiece bowl, framed wall art, sheer curtains.
* **Storage Units**: Sideboard console with felt-lined cutlery drawers and shelves for dinnerware, open glassware display shelving.
* **Materials & Finishes**: Solid oak, walnut, or sintered stone tabletop; bentwood or upholstered performance fabric chairs; brushed brass or antique bronze hardware.
* **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  * **60% Dominant**: Warm linen or warm ivory walls.
  * **30% Secondary**: Rich natural timber or warm taupe in table and chair frames.
  * **10% Accent**: Deep olive green, terracotta, or champagne brass in chair upholstery and lighting.
* **Ergonomics & Layout**: Minimum 90 cm (36") clearance between table edges and walls/sideboards for pushing chairs back and walking; Table sizing: 4-seater (1.2 m × 0.8 m), 6-seater (1.8 m × 0.9 m), 8-seater (2.4 m × 1.0 m).
* **Budget Alternatives**:
  * *Budget-Friendly*: Engineered oak veneer table, molded polypropylene chairs with wooden legs, multi-bulb pendant fixture.
  * *Premium / Luxury*: Live-edge solid walnut dining table, Italian leather dining chairs, designer hand-blown glass chandelier.

## Design Generation Rules & Engineering Guardrails

1. **Category Relevance Check**: Only items scientifically relevant to the selected room type are loaded into the candidate set.
2. **Dimension & Area Scaling**: Dining tables scale automatically by square meters (2-seater for <8 m², 4-seater for 8–15 m², 6-seater for 15–22 m², 8-seater for 22+ m²); beds scale from double to Queen or King based on width.
3. **Ergonomic Clearance Boundaries**:
   * Coffee table to sofa: **45 cm**.
   * Dining chair push-back: **90 cm**.
   * Kitchen aisle clearance: **105–120 cm**.
   * Bedside walkways: **65–75 cm**.
   * Primary entry and transit paths: **min. 75–90 cm**.
4. **SAT Collision & A* Circulation Validation**: Every recommended layout is checked with the Separating Axis Theorem (OBB 2D collision detection) and grid-based A* pathfinding to verify that all doors, windows, and furniture items remain reachable.
5. **Color & Lighting Formula**: Every room applies the **60-30-10 color rule** and a **3-tier lighting hierarchy** (Ambient, Task, and Accent) to guarantee aesthetic harmony and visual depth.
